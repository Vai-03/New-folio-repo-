About

I didn’t begin with screens. I began with questions.

01 · Origin

Curiosity became a craft.

What started as a hobby became a career when I found that design could make something feel beautiful and work better at the same time.

02 · The shift

From pixels to systems.

Enterprise and AI work taught me to look past the screen: at handoffs, business constraints, model uncertainty, and the people making the next decision.

03 · Now

I design for trust.

The detail I care about most is the one that helps a person understand what happened, what matters, and what they can do next.

My work lives where human behaviour, business intent and technical reality meet.
