Overview

What it is

Unvoro is an AI SEO and GEO platform. Its features were powerful but felt like separate tools; I reworked them into one flow, from keyword opportunity to a published, measurable post.

Unvoro’s SEO, GEO and AI-writing features were powerful but felt like separate tools. I reworked them into one flow, from keyword opportunity to a published, measurable post.

The problem

Why it needed design

Users landed on a dashboard full of metrics and actions but no clear next step.

SEO, GEO and AI-writing tools each had their own entry point, so people bounced between them and lost context.

The design question: how might we turn a toolbox into a guided path that a busy marketer can follow?

Users

Who we designed for

Proto-personas built from the product’s user roles and early conversations.

A

Aisha

Founder of a small D2C brand

“I don’t have time to learn SEO. Just tell me what to do this week.”

Goal: Grow organic traffic without hiring an agency

Frustration: Jargon; too many options; no clear priority

D

Dev

In-house content marketer

“I want the data, but in an order that makes sense.”

Goal: Plan, write and publish content that ranks

Frustration: Switching tools; losing track of what’s live

Research

What we found

Qualitative

  • 01New users didn’t know where to start; the dashboard showed numbers but not actions.
  • 02People wanted a monthly plan more than a list of every possible fix.
  • 03A Marketer and a Simple mode mapped to two real ways people wanted to work.
  • 04Seeing the result of a published post closed the loop and built trust in the tool.

Reflection

What I learned

01

An audit is only useful if it ends in a clear next step for the user, and for the team.

02

Modes can respect expertise without splitting the product in two.

03

Closing the loop with measurement is what makes an AI tool feel trustworthy.
