Project overview

Generic chat can make a model look more certain than it is. This interaction model treats confidence, sources, “needs review,” and escalation as part of the interface—not policy copy.

Objective

Make confidence, sources, “needs review,” and escalation part of the interface, so the assistant never looks more certain than it is.

Problem statement

Generic chat can make a model look more certain than it is, and policy copy alone does not keep the human in reach.

My role

Conversation model, safety and handoff patterns, high-fidelity prototype, and stakeholder deck.

Project focus

Clinical AI · Conversation design · Safety

02 / 04

Key insight

A model that sounds certain is a risk. Confidence, sources and the human handoff have to be part of the interface.
