Project overview

As the sole product designer, I shaped an AI aspiration engine alongside persona-specific dashboards—recommendations, goals, and progress without forcing every user into the same generic home.

Objective

Give students, staff and admins each a home built for their own job, with an AI aspiration engine that turns goals into recommendations and progress.

Problem statement

A single generic home would serve none of the three groups: their jobs, decisions and data are very different.

My role

Sole product designer · Research, IA, aspiration-engine UX, analytics visualisation, and the product design system.

Project focus

AI · EdTech · Design system
