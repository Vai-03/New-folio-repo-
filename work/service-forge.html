01 / 08

Project overview

Restaurant groups were running equipment servicing in chats and spreadsheets. I made the service ticket the centre of gravity: one request, many actors, and a visible state from issue to close.

Objective

Create a shared service workflow where restaurant teams and vendors can follow the same request and its status.

Problem statement

Service work was spread across chats and spreadsheets, leaving teams without one trusted view of a ticket’s progress.

My role

UX/UI Designer · Product concept, information architecture, interface system, and clickable prototype.

Project focus

Enterprise · Operations · Product system
