Legacy tools assumed a desk and mouse. I split fast field capture from supervisor oversight, keeping evidence, routing, and close-out connected through the full shift.

Objective

Keep evidence, routing, and close-out connected through a full shift, with a handheld flow that works for the person in the field.

My role
